# Compensation Policy (effective 1 September 2026)

## Lost or damaged items
Compensation for items lost or damaged in transit is capped at £50 per item. Higher amounts are only available if the customer bought Enhanced Cover when booking, which covers items worth up to £1,000.

## How to claim
Claims must be made within 14 days of the delivery date using the online claims form, with photos of the item and its packaging. The claims team assesses every claim and replies within 10 working days. Support agents cannot approve compensation in chat.
