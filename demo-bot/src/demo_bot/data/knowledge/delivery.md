# Delivery Services

## Delivery options
Example Parcels offers Standard delivery (2 to 4 working days, £3.99) and Next-Day delivery (order by 6pm, £7.99). We deliver Monday to Saturday across mainland UK.

## Tracking a parcel
Every order has a reference starting with EP-, for example EP-100245. Customers can track it on our website or app. Tracking updates within 2 hours of each scan.

## Late deliveries
If a parcel arrives more than 2 working days after its estimated delivery date, we refund the delivery charge. Support agents can issue this refund directly, up to a maximum of £15. A late delivery does not entitle the customer to any other compensation.

## Missed deliveries
If nobody is in, the driver leaves a card and tries again the next working day. After two attempts the parcel goes to the nearest pickup point, where it is kept for 7 days.
